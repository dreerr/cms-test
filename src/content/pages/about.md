Mira Vogt is an Austrian artist and researcher whose practice spans installation, sculpture, and documentary photography. Her work explores the meeting points between material culture, environmental data, and embodied knowledge — how industrial and ecological processes leave traces that can be read, translated, and misread.

She studied at the Universität für angewandte Kunst Wien (2013–2018, Studio Konrad Bayer) and holds a research fellowship at the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna. Her work has been presented at the Wiener Festwochen, steirischer herbst, Kunsthalle Wien, Haus der Kulturen der Welt (Berlin), MUDAM (Luxembourg), Fotomuseum Winterthur, and the Fotogalerie Wien, among others.
